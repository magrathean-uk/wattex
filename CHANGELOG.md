# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 25 October 2026

First release.

- Battery power in watts in the menu bar, refreshed every 0.5 s from the SMC.
- Battery icon in 5% steps, with a bolt while a charger is connected.
- Fixed-width layout: five monospaced characters, constant item length.
- Fallback to total system power on Macs without a battery (untested on hardware).
- Starts at login after the first launch.
